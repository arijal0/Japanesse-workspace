==============================================================================
J. Checks of statements in the paper against the released data/code
==============================================================================
1) Wald chi2 for model effect = 83.70 on 4 df  ->  chi2/df = 20.93
   Paper reports F(4, 8580) = 20.93. So the paper's F is the Wald chi2/df from a
   rater-only model; the paper text says scenario ID was also a random intercept,
   but the CSV has no scenario column and the notebook does not include it.

2) ICC from the LMM variance components = 0.342 / (0.342 + 0.394) = 0.464
   Paper says the 0.837 ICC was computed 'from the LMM's variance components', but
   0.837 actually comes from pingouin ICC2k averaged over dimensions (section D).
   The LMM quantity measures how much raters differ in overall leniency, not agreement.

3) Cronbach's alpha of the 5 items per dimension (rows = rater x model); the values
   are blank in the HTML version of the paper:
   PDI: alpha = 0.887
   IDV: alpha = 0.866
   UAI: alpha = 0.884
   MAS: alpha = 0.796
   LTO: alpha = 0.855
   IND: alpha = 0.865

4) Paper's Discussion mentions 'a 0.5-point difference on a 5-point scale' between top
   and lower models. Actual JWCAS range = 0.177.
